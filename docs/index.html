premium-selling

A same-day SPY option spread system on AWS: a morning regime check, spread entry, position checks every 5 minutes, a forced close at 15:45 Eastern and an end-of-day summary, each as a scheduled Lambda function.

Status: incomplete prototype for a paper trading account. The order lifecycle is tested offline only; the repository records no run against the broker or a deployed stack.

Problem

A same-day options position has to be watched from entry until it is closed. In this system that is from 10:00 to 15:45 Eastern, with a check every 5 minutes against a stop at 2 times the credit received and a take profit at half of it. A missed check leaves a position past its threshold.

This repository runs each step on a schedule in AWS. Positions and trades are kept in DynamoDB. A close is started for every open position at the session close deadline, 15:45 Eastern on a regular day and earlier on an early close, and a position is marked closed only after its close order fills. The handlers send trade and error alerts over SNS.

Schedules: cdk/stacks/orchestration_stack.py. Thresholds: src/common/config.py, src/monitor/handler.py.

How it works

The entry workflow runs the scanner for both strategies in parallel and calls the executor when the detected regime matches: an iron condor on range days, a credit spread with the trend on trend days. A second workflow runs the monitor. The closer and the reconciler are invoked by the scheduler directly.

Limitations

  • Entries and closes are confirmed from broker fills, but that handling is tested against offline fakes built from alpaca-py models only, not against the broker.
  • A close order that does not fill returns the position to open, and the monitor tries again on its next run. A position still active after the 15:45 closer is reported on the error topic and by the end-of-day reconciliation against the broker.
  • The SNS topics have a subscriber only when an alert address is given at deploy time.
  • The repository contains no backtest, no simulation and no trading record. Nothing in it shows that the strategies have an edge.

Details and file references: the README section "Design decisions and limitations".
